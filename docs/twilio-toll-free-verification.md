# Twilio toll-free verification: answers for BTC Scheduler

Use this when submitting the toll-free verification for the BTC Scheduler number in the Twilio Console (**Messaging > Regulatory Compliance > Toll-Free Verification**, or from the number's page). The number cannot text US or Canadian phones until Twilio approves it.

Fields marked **[fill in]** need company details that are not in this repository. Keep the wording consistent with the booking form and the texts below: reviewers compare the description, the opt-in and the samples.

## Opt-in workflow

**Description:**

> On the booking form at https://book.bigthinkcapital.com, after entering their phone number, the business owner sees an unchecked, optional checkbox: "Send me text message reminders about this meeting at this number." Below it: "Optional, and not required to book. If you check the box, you agree to receive automated reminder texts for this meeting at the number above. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time." Texts are sent only when the box is checked; the time of consent is stored with the booking. The form is also available in Spanish with the same wording. Replying STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, OPT OUT, or the Spanish ALTO, PARAR, BAJA, CANCELAR, DETENER) stops all texts; START opts back in.

**Screenshot or URL of the opt-in:** the checkbox appears only on booking pages that send merchant text reminders. Turn on merchant text reminders for one live page, then take a screenshot of the booking form with the checkbox and the disclosure visible, and host it at a public URL (or attach it if the form allows).

**Privacy policy URL:** **[fill in]** BTC's privacy policy. Carriers expect it to say that mobile numbers and text opt-in data are not shared with third parties for marketing.

## After approval

1. Set the number's **A message comes in** webhook to `POST https://book.bigthinkcapital.com/api/sms/twilio`.
2. In BTC Scheduler, **Admin > Settings > Text messages (Twilio)**: set the Account SID, auth token and sending number, then press **Send test text**.
3. Reply STOP to the test text, confirm the number is on the opt-out list, then reply START.

The template wording above comes from `messages/en.json`, `messages/es.json` (`sms`, `booking.smsConsent`, `booking.smsDisclosure`) and `src/server/sms/templates.ts`. If those change, update this page and the verification.
